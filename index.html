What we do

Everything here is built around the idea that language grows through regular contact, not perfect grammar drills.

あ

Conversation hours

Weekly small-group sessions split by level, so beginners are never lost and advanced speakers still have peers to push against.

読

Reading practice

Work through short stories, news snippets, and manga passages together, with context instead of isolated vocabulary lists.

書

Writing corner

Post a paragraph, get gentle corrections. Kanji, kana, and everything in between — corrections are always explained.

話

Native speaker exchange

Pair up with a fluent speaker for an hour. Both sides get the same benefit, and both sides learn something they didn't know.

曲

Media reviews

Anime, drama, music, and film recommendations with notes on what's learnable at your level and what's worth just enjoying.

語

Resource library

Curated free and low-cost materials, kept in one place and updated by members as they finish working through them.

How it works

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them. Nobody checks whether you finished the last one.

Pick your level

No test, no shame. Start where you actually are, move when it feels right.

Join a session

Sign up for a conversation hour that fits your week. Seats are small on purpose.

Practice in writing

Post when you're stuck. Corrections come with the reason, not just the fix.

Keep going

Consistency beats intensity. An hour a week for a year will change how you think.
